# ADR-0017: Community license

- **Status:** Owner-approved, subject to qualified legal review.
- **Source decision:** Community portion of original Ferrum architecture ADR-0017, baseline commit `1fba2e2ab599ee2952a0e7e10a6dba78a66bfb79`.
- **Scope:** Original materials that the Community project owner has authority to license.

## Decision

Community is provisionally published under the unmodified Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0). The repository's root `LICENSE` file contains the standard license text. This public declaration is operative for Covered Software carrying the required MPL notice; “provisional” refers to pending project legal review and does not alter the license terms.

This license record does not grant rights to trademarks, logos, domains, unrelated third-party materials or materials outside the covered Community scope. Third-party components retain their own license and notice requirements.

## Pending legal review

Qualified legal review must confirm ownership and authority to license project materials, inbound contribution terms, third-party provenance, patent and secondary-license implications, and applicable distribution terms before commercial launch. No CLA or DCO is currently represented as adopted.

## Consequences

Do not describe the license as a non-binding draft or as legally finalized. Any licensing-scope or license change requires a separate owner-approved decision and legal review. See [LICENSE-STATUS.md](../../LICENSE-STATUS.md) for the current operational checklist.
